# Product retouch example sources

These images are intentionally unpolished "before" photos: the product remains identifiable
and sufficiently clear, while lighting, color, background, or composition leaves visible room
for retouching. Production serves the trial copies from the dedicated OSS site-asset prefix
`site-assets/original/product-retouch-examples`; the upstream sources and licenses are recorded here.

- `running-shoe.jpg` — “Running Shoes on hardwood floor,” Tony Webster,
  [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Running_Shoes_on_hardwood_floor_(27833845501).jpg),
  [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).
- `perfume-bottle.jpg` — “Perfume Bottle,” ChickenFreak,
  [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Perfume_Bottle.jpg), public domain.
- `white-headphones.jpg` — “Headphones on desk,” Lirazelf,
  [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Headphones_on_desk.jpg),
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `smartwatch.jpg` — “Huawei Smartwatch (Band 4),” TheArchivistMan,
  [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Huawei_Smartwatch_(Band_4).jpg),
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `camera.jpg` — “Nikon camera on a table,” Tomwsulcer,
  [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nikon_camera_on_a_table.jpg),
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The files are served without editorial image alterations; only hosting/thumbnail resizing is used.
